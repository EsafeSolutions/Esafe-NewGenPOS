# All registers and payment tallies

Open **Register → All registers** to review open shifts across authorized POS Profiles. Managers see the open shifts they supervise; cashiers see their own open shifts. Each card shows net sales, returns, account sales and saved-change points. **Full summary / adjust / close** adds separate sales and return counts/amounts, discounts, debt collections, redeemed/earned points, expected payment totals and the cash equation. The same full summary is available on This register and under Previous sessions, alongside saved payment counts.

- **Switch register** uses the existing POS Profile switch and reloads the catalogue. A basket on screen requires confirmation before it is cleared. Per-cashier ownership and workstation restrictions continue to apply; switching the viewed register does not transfer another cashier's shift.
- **Adjust drawer cash** records positive cash in/out with a drawer and reason. The server enforces existing cash permissions and approvals and keeps the user/time in the cash movement log. Corrections do not rewrite invoices or the opening float.
- **Review and close all registers** opens each independent shift's payment counts. Upload offline sales on every device and stop selling there, count each payment, explain differences when required, and tick Reviewed. **Close all registers** closes reviewed shifts sequentially. Each successful close is durable; an error stops the remaining shifts and reports the completed number. Background ERPNext consolidation can still need attention under Previous sessions.
- Shifts sharing a physical drawer retain **Close all linked shifts** and one combined count. Open their full summary and finish the linked group first, then finish independent registers in the bulk review. Never count the same physical drawer twice. The bulk action remains disabled while linked drawers are still open or any required count/summary is missing.

Expected cash is **opening float + net cash payments + cash in − cash out**. Net payments include cash sales and debt collections, minus cash refunds and change. Count all physical notes and coins, including the float. Saved change remains physical cash and is already recorded as Cash In; do not add it again. Every foreign drawer has its own float, movements, currency and count.

Card expected totals are payments and collections less refunds for that method. Compare with the terminal/provider batch before fees. Bank transfer totals must match actual received transfers and refund debits, using references. Neither adds physical drawer cash. Account sales add customer debt and are excluded from received payment counts; subsequent collections enter the payment method used. Redeemed points pay for goods without adding drawer cash.

**Difference = counted − expected**: ✓ Tallied means equal, − Short means lower, + Over means higher. Enter zero explicitly for an empty method. Recount and check missing uploads, refunds, change and incorrect payment methods before explaining the difference. Use the existing return-and-corrected-sale workflow for payment-method mistakes before closing.

Bulk closing refreshes every shift before posting and submits invoice/collection counts plus a strict payment/currency snapshot. The server compares the snapshot while holding the shift lock, so a cash movement between review and closing cannot silently change the signed-off tally. Existing endpoints remain compatible with older tills. Permissions and offline checks apply on both sides; the new UI does not upload another device's private offline queue.
